<h1>Rules about the public service:</h1>
<em> </em>* You cannot destroy whole cities (townhall)<br>
<em> </em>* A city cannot build new buildings if it lacks some of the relevant building materials<br>
<em> </em>* When building new factories, you must check what is the maximum production allowed for that industry. This will be shown in the Progress tab of this window.  Unfortunatelly, the information may appear a few seconds after.  Once you adjust the production, click again on the same tile to confirm and build.<br>

We have the year {year} and the new Abbot Primate, a very forward thinking person,
of the Great Abbey has done his starting visit at the Arch-Abbot residence.
On his journey he took advantage of the new underground railway in the capital.
Excited from this new transport system he decided to take care to get a new
transportation system in his area.<br>
<br>
Since centuries the people around the abbey have traveled by feed, horse or horse-drawn carriage.
Nevertheless the last centuries in that rough landscape have been very successfull
for the Great Abbey, they own a few small villages with a impressive number of businesses.
But the most important is, that the Great Abbey itself is the cultural and economic
center of the greater urban area, with markets, dairies and pubs.<br>
<br>
Map facts at scenario start:<br>{map_info_start}
<br>
In the knowledge of the urban heritage conservation area around the Great Abbey
the Abbot Primate searched for a solution. He received the permission to
construct a huge underground station under the center of the Great Abbey.
There is only a small flaw, the other Abbots like to have some extra services at
this new station. Last but not least to increase the economic power of the Great Abbey
a trusty support with goods for all markets, dairies and pubs of the Great Abbey is required.<br>
<br>
The Abbot Primate also knows that money makes the world go round, so he did make a
deposit to the account of the new transportation company. Having his connections he
was mediated to you to solve all the transportation problems as the manager of this new company.<br>
